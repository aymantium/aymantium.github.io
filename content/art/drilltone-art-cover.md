The cover art for Drilltone Wisdom, the release featuring kesskass instrument, holds a really special place in my creative journey.

<iframe style="border: 0; width: 100%; height: 120px;" src="https://bandcamp.com/EmbeddedPlayer/album=2575271412/size=large/bgcol=ffffff/linkcol=0687f5/tracklist=false/artwork=small/transparent=true/" seamless><a href="https://ayman.bandcamp.com/album/drilltone-wisdom">Drilltone Wisdom by Aymantium</a></iframe>

The main inspiration for the angel came from the last scene in the [music video for Hayden Pedigo - Letting Go](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wZRxhPDz7mw). That song is so beautiful, and I really wanted to bring something visual like that into my own art.

At the time, I asked AI to generate the angel figure for me. I believed in using AI as a brush to help make a better creation and capture the idea I had in mind. For the background, I used a photo I took myself, putting it through many filters and edits to build up a deep multi vibe texture.

![the old cover](/assets/images/aymantium-drilltone-wisdom-Cover-Art.jpg)

My perspective has changed since then. I later became anti AI for my release cover art because I like the product to be fully mine from start to finish.

Even with that shift in my mindset, I still really like this piece. It marks an important moment in my process, and the result gave this multi vibe thing that I still appreciate today.
