# Vecraft’s Theme Electronix

Vecraft’s Theme Electronix (Vecraft’s Theme Electronic experiment) is an electronic release built around a signature that appeared in the start of the track Vecraft’s Thunder from Purification by Accident, The Last Dream from AYMANTIA... (and other songs).

In that earlier release, the signature was introduced in the opening track and played on guitar. a recognizable gesture that later found its way into other pieces. This new release takes that original idea and removes it from its initial form.

There is no guitar here. All sounds are electronic and originate from compositions written as code in Sonic Pi. These coded materials were recorded, sampled, and assembled in a DAW to form this tracks.

The signature becomes the main melodic and structural material of the release. It runs through the entire length of each piece, sometimes clearly stated, sometimes stretched, layered, or transformed.

Vecraft’s Theme Electronix is an experiment in persistence. One idea carried through every track, translated fully into electronic form.
