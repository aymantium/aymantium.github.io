# Under the Spell of Life

I came up with this songs after doodling a character in class named "SCP 097787".
This character feels vibrations using maggot-like sensors on his face. He doesn’t have eyes. His whole face is swollen, perforated, and underneath the skin he can move a long, diamond-shaped tongue around through the leathery, mummy-like tissue.

I was bored in class, drew him, and suddenly the idea of making a long track about him just appeared in my head. On the weekend I picked up my guitar, wrote some melodies, and the journey started there. I kept building it piece by piece in my DAW.

I recorded this music while I wasn’t in a great mood. Making this song became something that helped me deal with the pain inside, the existential, emotional, psychological, and social stuff that piles up.

This songs comes straight from my bedroom and straight from my heart.
The melody is my emotions speaking.
