# Mountain <A>

Heavy riffs collapse into noise which collapses into people having a very polite, very intelligent conversation.
One track features the kesskass, a handmade instrument run through distortion until it sounds like a moaning elephant recorded on a microphone from 1984.
Some vocals arrive through my bass pickups, because apparently the normal way of singing wasn't strange enough. Water, recorded with a piezo
