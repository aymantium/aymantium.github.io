# The Thing of Things

A collection of memories from an era that refuses to fade. News broadcasts, the internet , cracked games, shock videos, revolutions, strange humor, and the feeling of growing up while the world changed around you.
These tracks are echoes of a time that was chaotic, exciting, and unsettling—memories that still linger long after ...
