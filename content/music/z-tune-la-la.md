# Z Tune La La

Z Tune La La is an experimental electronic album built through live coding and studio reconstruction.

Some tracks started as raw ideas written in Sonic Pi and later sampled, arranged, and structured inside the DAW. Others were performed full or partially live-coded. The album moves between those two approaches: spontaneous execution and careful arrangement.

Across 18 tracks, the sound shifts between breakbeat, glitch, ambient passages, distorted textures, and rhythm-driven experiments. There are moments that are intense and chaotic, and others that are minimal and repetitive.

Tools used: Sonic Pi, Reaper, Python, Spek, GIMP
