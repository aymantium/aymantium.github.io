# The Eye Man Experiments

The Eye Man Experiments is a compilation of tracks made over time, mostly around 2022-2024 to Now, some songs never really found a proper release. others were finished and published under my other projects (Vecraft, MURGOD..)

This compilation serves as an archive of my experiments. it moves freely between punk, metal, dungeon synth, acoustic, ambient, and noise-driven ideas, treating genre as a tool rather than a boundary.

Track Origins :

- Forgotten : https://vecraft.bandcamp.com/album/closed-and-forgotten

- Eel Vomit Punk Party, Left-handed Factory, A Day in the Robots' Mines, Bend the Wire : https://vecraft.bandcamp.com/album/the-raft

- IFRAAN : https://murgod.bandcamp.com/album/ifraan

- Other Songs : my early material
