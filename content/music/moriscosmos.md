# Moriscosmos

Moriscosmos started with a dirt-cheap classical guitar that I repaired and fitted with a piezo pickup. What began as simple recordings slowly grew into a collection of pieces, joined by electric guitar/bass and electronic experimentation.

The distorted, black metal sections in "Trifles" and "Coffee Ink" were recorded entirely with that classical guitar and its piezo. Throughout the album, you'll also hear water sounds from the STS-17 synthesizer plugin, a sound I keep coming back to because I like to keep my music hydrated.

The record carries a traditional atmosphere as well, drawing inspiration from Gnawa music in "Post Gnawatic Disorder" and Ahidous in "The Protocols of the Elders of Zayan," inspired by the Zayan Amazigh people of Morocco's Central Middle Atlas.
